# Heaven Lands

A cozy pixel-art world to wander, build and grow in, alone or with friends. There is no fighting: you make a wanderer, dress them however you like, and go where you please. Every sprite and every sound is made in code. It runs in the browser and installs as an app on phones and computers.

## What there is

- **The creator**: body, face, hair, clothes, accessories and things to carry, with eight saved outfits.
- **The Atlas**: islands adrift on a sea of cloud. Tap one to see what's there, then Go, or Together to play with friends.
- **Hearthhome**: your own isle. Build cottages and tents, lay floors and bridges, plant a garden, cook, fish, and let the critters you've caught live there.
- **The Everwood**: a forest without an end, with brooks, campfires, shrines and old stones, and a white stag that comes to those who wander long enough. You can build there too.
- **Quiet places**: Cloudrest, the Sunken Garden, Glimmerdeep and Starwatch.
- **Endless lands**: Glowtide Shore, Saltglass Flats, Hushfall and Lumen Meadow, which go on as far as you care to walk.

Online, up to four people share a room by its 4-letter code.

## Controls

| | Touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk | joystick (left) | WASD |
| Emotes: wave, cheer, dance, sit, heart | buttons (bottom right) | 4 to 8 |
| Talk, harvest, swing the net | act button | E |
| Hotbar | tap a slot | 1 to 3 |
| Map | tap the minimap | Tab |
| Day and night | | N |

## Running it

`npm install`, then `npm run dev`. `npm run build` makes the static site in `dist/`.
